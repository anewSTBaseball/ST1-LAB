NOTE FROM MR. SANGABI — 200 HALL

I was on my way to the copy room when I saw a student rushing out 
with a handful of papers. They were all face-down, but one sheet slipped 
and I caught a glimpse of something strange.

It said students would have to “walk backwards all day” if they were tardy.
That can’t be real… can it?

The student bolted before I could ask questions. They dropped a scrap of paper 
with a partial web address on it — something about “tardy updates.”

Coach Rundell in the 300 Hall mentioned he received a weird email about new 
tardy punishments. Might want to check with him next.
